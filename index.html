🛡️ Safety & Etiquette

  • No helmet, no ride. No headphones. No alcohol mid-ride.
  • Ride single file or two-by-two — never three abreast.
  • Never overlap wheels. Hands on bars, fingers covering brakes.
  • Communicate obstacles, traffic and direction changes.
  • All rides are no-drop — regroup at junctions and at the top of climbs.
  • Stay right of the white line. Descend in your comfort zone.
  • If you have a problem, tell the group before you stop.

🍎 Nutrition & Hydration

  • Target 60 g carbs / hour on rides over 2 h
  • 500–750 ml fluid / hour, more in the heat
  • Add electrolyte tabs when sweating hard
  • ESC provides daily: 2 energy bars, 1 gel, 1 banana, 2 bottles to refill
  • Mid-ride lunch stop is included; coffee stops are not — bring a few €
  • Eat before you get hungry

🎒 Bring Yourself

  • Helmet (mandatory)
  • Cycling kit for 3 days + padded shorts
  • Cycling shoes & pedals (or normal shoes)
  • Waterproof jacket + warm layer
  • Sunglasses, suncream, after-sun
  • Personal medi-kit
  • Cash & card (€), passport / ID, GHIC
  • Phone (charged) + waterproof cover
  • Bike computer (optional)
  • Cycling gloves & saddle bag (optional)

ESC provides: rented bike, pump, tyre levers, allen key, 2× bottles, jersey (subject to stock).

The "Beast of Provence" rises to 1,910m. The climb from Bédoin is 21.5km at 7.5% average. The last 6km above the tree line are legendary.
